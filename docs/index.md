# Follow The Tune

**Learn the songs you actually want to play.**

Import your own MIDI files and practice them on your piano with real sheet music, falling notes and instant feedback. No subscription and no per-song fees.

[Support]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}) · [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

## Features

- **Your own songs:** import MIDI files and ABC notation, as many as you like
- **Three ways to practice:** Wait for me, Play along and Listen
- **One hand or both:** the app can play the other part
- **Tempo from 25 to 150 percent,** metronome and A-B loop
- **Real sheet music and falling notes,** with lit keys and instant feedback on every note
- **Stars and scores** for every song, and printable letter sheets
- **MIDI over USB or Bluetooth,** or the on-screen keyboard
- **Private:** no account, no ads, no data collection, and it works offline

Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Works best with a digital piano or keyboard with MIDI.
